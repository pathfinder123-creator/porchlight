New to Volunteering?

A few simple steps to help you get started.

Whether you're interested in helping at a food pantry, library, animal shelter, hospital, community event, or somewhere else, you can use these steps to find a good starting point.

  1. Start with what interests you

    Think about the activities you enjoy. Do you like working with people, solving problems, creating things, organizing details, or working with your hands? Volunteering is a chance to use your interests in ways that benefit others.

    Not sure? Porchlight's Interest Assessment can help you explore.

  2. Consider what works for you

    Think about how much time you can offer, how far you're willing to travel, and whether you'd prefer an in-person, virtual, or flexible opportunity. Some organizations welcome occasional help, while others ask for a regular commitment.

  3. Explore an opportunity

    Read about volunteer activities that appeal to you. Visit the organization's website to check current openings, age requirements, training, applications, and other expectations. These details vary by organization.

  4. Make the first connection

    Follow the organization's instructions to apply, register, or express your interest. If something isn't clear, ask about the schedule, duties, training, accessibility, or what to expect. You don't need to know everything before reaching out.

  5. Prepare for your first experience

    Confirm when and where to arrive, what to wear or bring, and whom to contact. Be punctual, follow the organization's guidance, respect confidentiality and safety procedures, and be ready to learn.

Good questions to ask before volunteering
  • What activities will I be doing?
  • Is training or orientation provided?
  • Is there a minimum time commitment?
  • Are there age or eligibility requirements?
  • What should I wear or bring?
  • Who can I contact about accessibility or accommodations?

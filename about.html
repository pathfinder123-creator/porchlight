About Porchlight

Porchlight helps people discover organizations and volunteer activities across Dallas and Collin counties and nearby communities. You can browse directly or use an optional activity-interest assessment.

Make a Difference. Build Your Experience.

Volunteering is an opportunity to serve your community while discovering your strengths, developing new skills and building meaningful relationships. For students and individuals exploring career pathways, volunteer experiences can also provide valuable hands-on learning, strengthen a résumé and help clarify future educational and professional goals. Porchlight helps you discover opportunities that align with your interests, whether you’re looking to give back, explore a career, gain experience or simply find a meaningful way to spend your time.

Install Porchlight on your device

Porchlight also works as a home-screen app. Installation is optional; you can always use it in your browser.

  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): Open Porchlight in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen and confirm.
  • Android (Chrome): Open Porchlight in Chrome, open the browser menu, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen (wording varies).
  • Desktop (Chrome or Edge): Open Porchlight and use the browser's Install option if available.

After visiting the site online, previously cached pages and volunteer listings may be available offline. Organization websites, applications, and current availability still require an internet connection. Reconnect and reload Porchlight to check for updated listings.

Privacy

Porchlight is designed without accounts. Assessment responses, saved opportunities, and discovery preferences are stored locally in your browser rather than submitted to a Porchlight server. Visiting an organization's website is subject to that organization's privacy practices.

Availability

Porchlight helps you discover organizations and volunteer activities that may interest you. Volunteer availability, application requirements, schedules, and eligibility can change. Visit the organization's website for the latest information before making plans.

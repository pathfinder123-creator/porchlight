About Porchlight

Porchlight helps people discover organizations and volunteer activities across Dallas and Collin counties and nearby communities. You can browse directly or use an optional activity-interest assessment.

Privacy

Porchlight is designed without accounts. Future assessment responses, saved opportunities, and discovery preferences will be stored locally in your browser rather than submitted to a Porchlight server. Visiting an organization's website is subject to that organization's privacy practices.

Availability

Porchlight helps you discover organizations and volunteer activities that may interest you. Volunteer availability, application requirements, schedules, and eligibility can change. Visit the organization's website for the latest information before making plans.

This is the initial site foundation. Assessment and directory functionality are not yet active.
